## Decisions that are the owner's (not taken here)

1. **Does a post-meal loop enter the plan before investors?** The cheapest honest version stores only what the person types: an optional reading and the time, next to the meal in the Diary, with no advice and no device. It is new health data (what to store about a person), so it needs the owner's decision and, before commercial use, a privacy line. A device integration is a different size of work.
2. **Medicines, exercise, physicians' recommendations:** a direction for the investor story (`docs/PRD-post-course.md`), not a feature to start. Medicines touch the dosing boundary.
3. **The tone of "no".** Options that keep design rule 6: lead with the swap, and name what would make it fit, not only what does not. A product decision, not a code change yet.
4. **Where users come from now:** the cohort first. A short page with a video link and "free, sign in with your email" is the gap.

## Where to find / how to reach
- University profile pages and Google Scholar (email is usually on the institution page).
- ResearchGate / LinkedIn (many RDN CDCES practitioners are active on LinkedIn).
- Conferences: ADA Scientific Sessions (June), ADCES annual (August), Diabetes Technology Society meeting (Nov), FNCE (Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, Oct).
- Organizations to ask for referrals: Glycemic Index Foundation (glycemicindex.com), INQUIS (inquis.com), Diabetes Technology Society (diabetestechnology.org), ADCES, Academy's Diabetes DPG.

## Suggested outreach order
1. Tier A in groups D and A first (Della Corte ×2, Atkinson, Chiavaroli, Trajanov, Eftimov) — they work on exactly the ingredient→GI/data problem and are junior enough to answer.
2. Tier A practitioners in group B (MacLeod, Lau, Davidson, Saslow, Franz, Warshaw, Smithson, Weisenberger) — they judge safety rules, onboarding branching by medication, and whether an RDN would recommend it.
3. Tier B/C — via a warm intro or a conference; ask one precise question, not "what do you think".

## What to ask (one question per expert, mapped to PRD risks)
- Group A: is per-ingredient GI aggregation into a dish GI defensible, and what error bars would you put on it?
- Group B: which medication/comorbidity branches must block or soften advice (insulin, SU, SGLT2, GLP-1, CKD)?
- Group C: what would make you trust a population-GI tool despite inter-individual variability?
- Group D: how did you validate LLM-assigned GI values, and what failure modes did you see?

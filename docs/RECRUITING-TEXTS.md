## E. What to say in a thread (comments during the warm-up)

Answer the question that was asked, from what you know. Say "this is my experience, not advice" and "ask your care team about medicines". If a post is about food logging or glycemic index and the app is truly relevant, add one sentence of disclosure (`GROWTH-5-100-launch.md`, section 1.3), never the first day and never twice in one thread.

## F. Do not

- Write "AI-powered" in the first line, promise a result ("lowers sugar", "reverses insulin resistance"), or say anything about metformin that reads as a recommendation. Your metformin is a personal fact, as in the texts above.
- Mention PCOS as something the app handles: it does not treat it separately. Say it as your own story only.
- Make a second account, ask friends to comment, or post the same text in several communities on the same day.

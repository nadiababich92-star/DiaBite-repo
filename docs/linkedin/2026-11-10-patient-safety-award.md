# 2026-11-10 — "Best Patient Safety AI Design" award (Agentic AI Institute, Cohort 10)

## Post (thank-you version)

I'm so happy to share that I received "Best Patient Safety AI Design" at the Agentic AI Institute, Cohort 10. 🏆

During this course I built DiaBite, a nutrition assistant for type 2 diabetes. Safety was the first design decision: an engine does the arithmetic, and a verifier blocks any number the tools didn't return. Being recognised for that choice means a lot to me.

Thank you, Mahesh Yadav, for the structure, the pushback and the standard you set. Thank you to the whole Agentic AI Institute team, and to my cohort for the sharp questions.

DiaBite is still a prototype, not medical advice, and I learn something from everyone who tries it. 💚

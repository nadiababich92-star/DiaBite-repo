Section one

Summary

DiaBite answers one question for people with type 2 diabetes, prediabetes or insulin resistance — can I eat this, how much, and with what? — from a sentence of plain English. A model reads the meal and plans the work; a deterministic engine computes every number; a verifier rejects any answer containing a figure no tool returned.

Why this is a Dutch plan, not the American one converted

  1. A smaller first round, and a team sized to it. €1.6M is a large Dutch pre-seed, not a small US seed. The hiring plan reaches 7 people at month 12 and 19 at month 36, against 12 and 26 in the US plan. The product does not need more — it is already built.
  2. WBSO. The Dutch R&D payroll-tax credit takes €252k off the personnel line across three years, modelled at 10% of payroll to verify — roughly the difference between a Dutch and a Polish cost base.
  3. Euro costs against dollar revenue. Azure bills inference in euros, so the measured cost of goods needs no conversion at all. Revenue is US direct-pay at US list prices.

On the residence question, stated plainly because it drives the timing. The Dutch startup visa is granted on the strength of an approved facilitator and an innovative product, not on having raised money to verify — IND and RVO. It does, separately, require evidence of sufficient means of support, which a founder salary from a funded company satisfies. So money is a requirement of the permit, not its mechanism: the facilitator is the gate, and the round is what makes living here possible once through it.

The innovation test is the part already strong. RVO assesses a technical claim; this one is measured — a deterministic engine, a verifier, 285 evaluation cases and a 100% verified rate on the most recent live run.

The question a Dutch investor asks first: why the US and not Europe?

Because the US has the largest diagnosed population in any English-speaking market, a direct-pay culture for health products, and a category — ZOE, Levels, Nutrisense — that has already taught consumers what a glycemic response is. We enter an aware market rather than creating one. ZOE is a British company selling heavily into the United States; the pattern is established.

Europe is a deliberate second market, not a skipped first one: it fragments by language, reimbursement and clinical convention, and the safety rules would need redoing per country.

Three roles that look discretionary and are not. The Registered Dietitian from month 1 makes the food data defensible — every record with a source and a verification date — and signs off that a recommendation is safe. The US medical director is a US-market requirement: clinical credibility there is held by US-licensed clinicians, and this is the one role deliberately paid at a US rate. Nutrition-data operations exists because the glycemic layer is a moat, and a moat nobody maintains stops being one.

WBSO reduces this line by €252k over three years, modelled as 10% of payroll. The statutory credit applies only to qualifying R&D hours, so the blended effect on total payroll is what is modelled; the real figure needs an application and an hours administration to verify — RVO.

Section ten

Risks

Regulatory

changes the plan, not the date

FDA general-wellness guidance covers products that promote a healthy lifestyle. A can I eat this verdict aimed at people with a diagnosis sits close to the line. If it falls outside, DiaBite becomes a regulated device: a 510(k) route would add an estimated $400k–900k and 12–18 months, which this plan does not carry. Mitigation: counsel engaged in month 2 — deliberately the first material spend — claims held inside general wellness until answered, a regulatory lead from month 14, a US medical director from month 15.

Two jurisdictions at once

A Dutch BV serving US consumers carries GDPR and US rules together, and the founder's residence depends on the entity performing. Mitigation: a device-scoped anonymous identity first, with deletion and export from the first migration; counsel's month-2 scope covers both; SOC 2 Type I in year two.

Residence timing

Every date here assumes the startup visa is granted. A facilitator is the gate and the application takes months to verify — IND. Mitigation: the facilitator search runs in parallel with the raise, and the work continues from Poland in the meantime — the product is already remote.

Acquisition cost

€107 against a €265 LTV is 2.5× in year one — workable, not comfortable, and the number most likely to disappoint. Mitigation: the clinician and search channels are built before paid is opened, which is why marketing is 26% of the pre-seed and not more.

Retention

Manual food logging has a documented adherence collapse, and every figure here rests on logging taking under 20 seconds. The clearest outside feedback received so far, from a diabetes educator on 20 October, was exactly this: people stop after about two months. Mitigation: the product is positioned as a decision, not a diary; the gates answer it in the first two cohorts.

One builder until the round closes

Mitigation: the first three hires close in months 1–3.

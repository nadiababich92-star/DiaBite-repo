Architecture

A model reads the sentence. It never does the sums.

The promise of the product, enforced by the structure: a language model decides what to look up, a deterministic engine does the arithmetic, and a verifier refuses any answer whose numbers did not come from the engine.

The app

React and TypeScriptAsk, Diary, Menu, You. Signed in, the diary and profile follow you across devices.
Safety rules firstDosing, red-flag symptoms, fasting and referral questions are caught by plain rules before any model runs, and before any rate limit.

Azure AI Foundry · agents

RouterA small model that sends the question to the right agent.
Meal agentCalls tools. Reads the foods, picks the tool, writes the answer in plain words.
AdvisorNo tools and no numbers. Answers “is keto sensible?” from our own documents and remembers food preferences.

Engine · Azure Container App

Toolsresolve_foods · compute_meal · cost_dish · get_day_state · find_alternatives
Foods, in Supabase8,633 records and 2,000 recipes, searched by meaning (pgvector) and by name.
VerifierEvery number in the answer must appear in a tool result, or the answer is not shown.
  1. You describe a meal. The safety rules read it first; most refusals never reach a model.
  2. The router picks the meal agent or the advisor.
  3. The meal agent resolves each food against the database. A match it is unsure of becomes a question, never a guess.
  4. The engine computes carbohydrate, glycemic index, glycemic load and what is left of your day.
  5. The verifier checks the answer against those results. If a number is not there, the model is asked once more, and then a plain template from the tool results is shown instead.
What it does now

More than the pitch showed

Ask in your own words

A verdict, three figures, a one-line reason and one next step, with the receipt one tap away.

Dishes it does not have

A family recipe or a restaurant plate is counted from typical ingredients and labelled an estimate. Change a gram or take an ingredient out and the engine counts it again.

Foods with no glycemic index

Packaged and survey foods, such as pizza or candy, show carbohydrate, fibre and calories. Their load is not invented, and the answer says so.

A diary that stays

Sign in and your profile and diary are there on any device. The Diary shows your week: how many of the last seven days stayed in range, and four weeks as a strip.

A weekly menu and shopping list

Seven days within your targets, drawn from 2,000 recipes, with single-meal swaps. Built and not yet reviewed by a clinician.

Your data, yours to delete

Delete your account in the app and the profile and diary go with it. Nothing about you is sent to a model beyond the meal and the budget.

What it is not

Limits, stated plainly

  • DiaBite is a reference tool, not medical advice. It does not suggest insulin or medicine doses, and a question about them gets a fixed refusal.
  • No clinician has signed it off yet. The reasoning behind each decision is written down for review.
  • The glycemic index is a published average for a food, not a measurement of your body. A day counted “in range” is based on what you log.
  • It is being prepared for its first user sessions. It is not launched, and some things will be wrong.

# Azure: the free trial ends on 21 October 2026

**Written:** 12 October 2026 · **Status:** needs the owner's action before 21 October · **Companion:** `docs/ROADMAP-to-investors.md` items 1.7 and 1.8

## What is happening

The subscription is `Azure subscription 1`, offer `FreeTrial_2014-09-01`, spending limit **on**. A free trial gives about $200 of credit for 30 days across everything in it, and the owner reports it ends on **21 October**. When the credit runs out or the 30 days pass, Azure **disables** the subscription (checked against Microsoft's documentation and community answers on 12 October; the exact timing for this account is in the owner's portal).

Everything DiaBite runs sits in this one subscription: the Container App `diabite-engine` (the app and the engine), the container registry, and the Foundry resource with the three agents (`diabite-triage`, `diabite-meal`, `diabite-advisor`). **If it is disabled, the app and the agents stop answering.** What does not depend on Azure: the code (GitHub), the food and account data (Supabase, its own free project), and the documents.

Microsoft's documentation says a disabled subscription is read-only and its resources stay for a while (up to 90 days for disabled subscriptions in general; a free trial may be shorter), and that **upgrading to pay-as-you-go re-enables them**. Some forum answers describe free-trial resources being decommissioned. Treat the upgrade as the way to be safe, not as something to do after the app stops.

## Recommended order

1. **Upgrade to pay-as-you-go this week (A)**, before 21 October, and **set a budget alert** at about €50 a month.

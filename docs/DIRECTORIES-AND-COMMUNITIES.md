# Where to list DiaBite, and where not to post (21 October 2026)

*Written for the owner. Sources are web searches of 21 October; several are blogs, and review times and free-tier rules change, so each line says "check the page". Nothing here has been submitted.*

**What these are for.** A link from another site is the strongest thing that lifts a new domain in search. A free directory gives a small amount of traffic and a link; it will not bring users by itself. Write a different description for each (copied text counts for nothing), and never pay for a "fast track".

**What we cannot measure.** The site has no analytics, by decision (ROADMAP 2.5), so we cannot tell which listing sent whom. Google Search Console shows which sites link to us after a few weeks (Links), and that is enough.

| Place | What it is | Note |

## Tier 2: after the sessions (needs a number or a quote to show)

| Place | Why it waits |
|---|---|
| Product Hunt | One shot at a launch day; it needs a short video (we have it), a tagline and people to vote. Launch when there are real users' words to show |
| BetaList | Free review can take months (about two, by one source); the paid fast track is not worth it |
| AlternativeTo | Needs competitors named honestly; after the competitor slide is final |

## Tier 3: health places (the audience, so ask before posting)

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| ADCES diabetes technology site (DANA) | Curated by ADCES staff; no public submission process was found. Write to ADCES and ask how a product is considered. The review used to cover clinical content, privacy and usability, so not before the clinicians have read us |
| TuDiabetes, Diabetes Daily | Their rules on promotion were not found in the search. Read the rules page of each; write to a moderator first; never post a link first |
| Beyond Type 2 (T'ara Smith) | A warm route through Melanie Batchelor (Beyond Type 1), after Melanie replies |
| Facebook groups for type 2 and PCOS | Research found about a quarter of posts in diabetes groups are promotion, and groups know it. Ask the admin; join and be useful for a few weeks first |

## Not now

- **Reddit:** dropped by the owner on 21 October.
- **Paid ads for a health app:** not in the plan; the platforms restrict health claims.
- **Mass directory lists (dozens of sites):** low value, and some are link farms. A handful of good places beats them.

## Rules for every listing

1. The description says what it is: a reference tool; it never gives a dose; not medical advice.
2. No claim that a clinician has reviewed it (no one has).
3. The address is `https://diabite.pro/` (the page is the same for `www`; the canonical tag points to the bare address).

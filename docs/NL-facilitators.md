## 1. How the route is actually shaped

- The startup residence permit is **temporary and valid for 1 year**. You start
  the company during that year.
- **One of the conditions is working with a facilitator** — a business mentor.
  The entrepreneur and the facilitator must put the partnership in a **signed
  agreement**.
- The application goes to the **IND**, not to RVO. RVO owns the facilitator
  recognition and the innovation assessment; IND grants the permit.
- **After a year** you apply for a *Residence permit self-employed person*. You
  either get a statement from your facilitator, or meet the standard
  self-employment requirements.

**Therefore:** the facilitator is a gate you pass *before* the permit, and the
facilitator's statement is what makes year two straightforward. Choosing one is
a two-year decision, not a formality.

## 2. What RVO requires of a facilitator

Quoted conditions, which are also a checklist for vetting one:

- Has **experience guiding innovative startups**.
- Is **financially stable** — not in receivership, not bankrupt, no negative equity.
- **May not hold a majority interest** in the startup.
- **May not be a family member** up to the third degree.

The facilitator provides "a tailor-made package of support depending on the
specific needs of the startup entrepreneur" — RVO names operational management,
marketing, research and investment acquisition as examples. The package is
negotiated, not fixed.

## 5. What to ask every one of them

The same five questions, so the answers are comparable:

1. **Do you take consumer health products selling into the US?** The combination
   is unusual and is the thing most likely to disqualify you quietly.
2. **What does the support package contain, and what does it cost?** RVO says it
   is tailor-made. Ask for the written scope and the fee.
3. **Do you take equity, and how much?** An accelerator and a workspace are very
   different answers.
4. **Is physical presence required, and where?** Several are campus-bound.
5. **On what basis do you issue the year-two statement?** This is the question
   that decides whether year two is routine or a second fight.

## 6. What is still missing before this is actionable

1. **The IND income requirement** — the monthly amount that counts as sufficient
   means of support, and whether a founder salary from your own BV qualifies.
   **This is the gap that matters most**, because it sets the minimum the round
   has to pay you. Read it at ind.nl, not from a summary.
2. **Application fees and processing times** at IND.
3. **Whether the innovation assessment happens before or after the facilitator
   agreement**, and what evidence RVO wants. The measured evaluation results —
   285 cases, verified rate 100%, the deterministic engine and verifier — are
   the strongest material available for that assessment, so the order matters.
4. **Nationality-specific steps** (MVV requirement, which depends on your
   passport).
5. **Whether any facilitator has taken a founder in a regulated health category
   before** — worth asking them for a reference.
